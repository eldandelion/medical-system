# Interview Gaps: Role-Specific Recent Activity

## 1. Clinical Staff Roles (Doctor, Head Councillor, Trial Admin)
- **Resolved (Doctor):** Referrals waiting for appointment or feedback, plus important unread notifications.
- **Resolved (Head Councillor):** Referrals awaiting triage, high-risk assessment submissions, and system-wide unread notifications.
- **Resolved (Trial Admin):** Referrals waiting for scheduling, unread notifications, and hospital staff capacity alerts.

## 2. Educational Roles (Teacher, Student)
- **Resolved (Teacher):** High-risk assessment submissions from their assigned students, and referral status updates for their assigned students.
- **Resolved (Student):** Status changes on their own referrals (e.g., scheduled, triaged), and pending assigned assessments they need to take.

## 3. Administrative Roles (System Admin)
- **Resolved (System Admin):** User registrations pending approval (defaulted).
